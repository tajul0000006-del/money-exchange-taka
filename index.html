Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to your most common questions

How does MoneyExchangeTaka work?
Select your sending and receiving payment methods, enter the amount and required details, then submit your exchange request. Review the rate and payment instructions before continuing.
Where can I view the exchange rates?
View the available rates in the Exchange Rates section. Check the exchange rate and applicable fees before confirming your order.
Which payment methods are supported?
Supported methods include Perfect Money, WebMoney, Binance USDT, KuCoin USDT, BingX USDT, Bybit USDT, ePay, Volet, RedotPay, bKash Personal, Nagad Personal, Rocket Personal, Upay Personal, and other methods displayed on the website.
How can I track my exchange request?
Use the Tracking Code provided after submission to check your status in the Request Tracking section.
What do Pending and Approved mean?
Pending means your request is waiting for review or processing. Approved means the request has been approved. Check the final status for confirmation that the transaction is complete.
HELP CENTER

More Questions & Answers

Find the information you need

How do I submit an exchange request?
Enter your payment method, amount, and required account details. Review the displayed instructions and submit the form.
What should I do if I enter the wrong wallet address?
Contact Support as soon as possible. Funds sent to an incorrect address or network may not be recoverable.
Which network should I use to send USDT?
Use only the network specified in your payment instructions. Verify that the network matches the wallet address.
How can I check the exchange fee?
Review the exchange rate, network fee, and service charge before confirming your order. Fees may vary by payment method.
Can I use a Binance Pay ID?
If available, use the Binance Pay ID shown in the payment instructions. Check applicable fees and limits before confirming.
How do I submit payment proof?
Enter the Transaction ID or reference number and attach a payment screenshot when requested.
Can I view multiple orders with the same Tracking Code?
Use the unique Tracking Code provided for each request.
How can I cancel or change an order?
Contact Support promptly to ask about your order status and whether a change is possible.
How can I contact Support?
Use the contact methods listed in the Support section of the website.
How can I keep my information safe?
Never share your password, OTP, or recovery phrase. Be careful when sharing payment account and tracking information.
