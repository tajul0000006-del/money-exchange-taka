UNDERSTANDING DIGITAL MONEY

USD and USDT. Similar Value, Different Money.

Both are commonly linked to the US dollar, but they are not the same kind of money. Their structure, transfer systems and risks are different.

$FIAT CURRENCY

US Dollar (USD)

The official currency of the United States, used in cash, banking and international trade.

TYPENational fiat currency
ISSUING SYSTEMUnited States monetary system
TRANSFERCash, banks and payment services
BLOCKCHAINNot a blockchain token
A USD balance shown by an online wallet or service is generally an account balance or claim recorded by that provider—not physical cash in your hand.
RELATED VALUENot interchangeable
₮DIGITAL ASSET

Tether (USDT)

A stablecoin issued by Tether and designed to track the value of the US dollar. It exists as a token on supported blockchain networks.

TYPEDollar-linked digital token
ISSUERTether
TRANSFERSupported blockchain networks
NETWORKMust match exactly
USDT is not a US government-issued currency or a bank deposit. Availability, redemption terms and supported networks depend on the provider.
Why the difference matters

USDT is designed to maintain a value close to USD, but its market price can move away from that target. It also carries issuer, platform, custody and blockchain-transfer risks. A transfer sent on the wrong network may be difficult or impossible to recover.

Always confirm the exact asset, network, exchange rate and receiving details before making a transfer. This information is general education, not financial or investment advice.
