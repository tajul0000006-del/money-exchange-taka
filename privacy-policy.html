Privacy Policy

This policy describes the types of information that may be handled when you use FastMoney, submit an exchange request, create an account or contact support.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the feature you use, information may include your name or account identifier, email address, contact method and contact details, selected payment methods, wallet or payment account details, requested amounts, transaction references, tracking codes, messages to support and payment proof images you choose to upload.

2. How We Use Information

We use submitted information to receive and review exchange requests, verify payments, process or track transactions, respond to support questions, maintain account access, prevent misuse and improve the reliability of the platform. We may also use request records to resolve disputes and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

3. Payment Credentials

Do not submit your wallet PIN, password, OTP, private key, recovery phrase or full account login credentials. FastMoney does not need these secrets to review an exchange request. If someone asks you for them while claiming to represent FastMoney, do not share them.

4. Service Providers

Some website functions may rely on third-party infrastructure providers for authentication, database hosting, file storage or delivery, and website hosting. Information necessary for those functions may be processed by the relevant provider under its own privacy and security terms. We do not publish private account or transaction information for advertising purposes.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when needed to complete a requested transaction with the relevant payment service, operate the website through service providers, respond to a valid legal request, protect users or investigate suspected fraud or abuse. We aim to limit shared information to what is reasonably necessary.

6. Storage and Retention

Request, account and support records may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, maintain transaction history, handle disputes, prevent fraud and comply with applicable requirements. Retention periods may differ by record type and will be reviewed as the service develops.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the platform. However, no website, device, network or online storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your account credentials private, use a secure device and contact support if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit information required for a particular request, but that may prevent us from processing it. If you want to ask about or correct information associated with a request, contact support and provide enough information to identify the request safely. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children and Third-Party Services

FastMoney is not intended for use by anyone who is not legally permitted to use the relevant financial or payment services. Third-party wallets and payment providers have their own privacy policies and terms; review them before using those services.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as FastMoney adds features or changes its data practices. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date where appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to information submitted through FastMoney, contact us through the support options displayed on the website. Include your tracking code only when it is relevant, and never send passwords, PINs or OTPs.

This policy is a general description of current website workflows, not legal advice. It should be reviewed and finalized against the actual production data flows and applicable privacy laws before launch.
