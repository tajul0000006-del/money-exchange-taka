01

Information We May Collect

Depending on the feature you use, information may include your name or account identifier, email address, contact method and contact details, selected payment methods, wallet or payment account details, requested amounts, transaction references, tracking codes, support messages and payment proof images you choose to upload.

02

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to receive and review exchange requests, verify payments, process or track transactions, respond to support questions, maintain account access, prevent misuse and improve platform reliability. Request records may also help resolve disputes and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

03

Payment Credentials

Do not submit your wallet PIN, password, OTP, private key, recovery phrase or full account login credentials. FastMoney does not need these secrets to review an exchange request. If someone asks for them while claiming to represent FastMoney, do not share them.

04

Service Providers & Sharing

Website functions may rely on third-party infrastructure for authentication, database hosting, file storage, delivery or website hosting. Information needed for those functions may be processed by the relevant provider under its own privacy and security terms. We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared when needed to complete a requested transaction, operate the website, respond to a valid legal request, protect users or investigate suspected fraud or abuse. We aim to limit sharing to what is reasonably necessary.

05

Storage, Retention & Security

Request, account and support records may be retained as reasonably needed to operate the service, maintain transaction history, handle disputes, prevent fraud and comply with applicable requirements. Retention periods may differ by record type. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website, device, network or online storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a secure device and contact support if you suspect unauthorized access.

06

Your Choices & Third-Party Services

You may choose not to submit information required for a particular request, but that may prevent us from processing it. To ask about or correct information associated with a request, contact support and provide enough information to identify it safely. We may need to verify your identity. Third-party wallets and payment providers have their own privacy policies and terms; review them before use. FastMoney is not intended for anyone who is not legally permitted to use the relevant financial or payment services.

07

Policy Updates & Contact

This policy may be updated as FastMoney adds features or changes its data practices. The latest version will be published on this page. For privacy questions, contact us through the support options displayed on the website. Include a tracking code only when relevant, and never send passwords, PINs or OTPs.
