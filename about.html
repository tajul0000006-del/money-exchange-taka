ABOUT MONEYEXCHANGETAKA

A clearer way to
manage digital exchanges.

MoneyExchangeTaka is a Bangladesh-focused digital exchange platform designed to make exchange requests easier to understand, submit and follow. We bring supported payment methods and exchange information together in one straightforward place.

01 / WHO WE ARE

About MoneyExchangeTaka

MoneyExchangeTaka aims to provide a simple and organised way for people to request exchanges between supported digital payment methods and currencies. The platform is being developed with a focus on clear information, a user-friendly experience and a request process customers can follow from submission to status updates.

We believe an exchange service should explain what information is needed, make applicable details easy to review and help customers understand what happens next.

02 / OUR EXCHANGE SYSTEM

A request-based, manually reviewed process

MoneyExchangeTaka follows a request-based exchange workflow. When a customer submits a request, its details are reviewed before the exchange is processed. This manual review is intended to check the selected methods, amount, payment information and applicable instructions.

Submitting a request does not by itself mean that an exchange has been approved or completed. The final rate, fees, payment instructions and availability must be confirmed for the specific request before proceeding.

03 / OUR SERVICES

Digital payment methods and currencies

The platform is designed to support exchange requests involving local digital wallets and selected currencies. Available methods may change as services are configured and maintained.

  • bKash
  • Nagad
  • Rocket
  • Upay
  • ePay and TunePay
  • USD and other currencies when enabled

Only methods currently displayed and enabled on the website should be considered available. Always review the live exchange information and instructions before making a payment.

04 / HOW IT WORKS

From exchange request to status update

Step 1 — Choose your exchange

Select the sending and receiving methods shown as available, then enter the amount you want to exchange.

Step 2 — Provide the required details

Enter accurate contact and payment information. Carefully check wallet numbers, account details, currency and network information.

Step 3 — Submit your request

Review the information and submit the exchange request. Keep the tracking reference provided for your request.

Step 4 — Wait for manual review

The request details are checked against the applicable exchange information and payment instructions. The team may contact you if clarification is needed.

Step 5 — Follow the request status

Use the available tracking feature or the support channel shown on the website to follow up. Treat an exchange as complete only when its final status confirms completion.

05 / MISSION & VISION

Making digital exchange easier to understand

Our Mission

Our mission is to make digital exchange requests more accessible through clear instructions, understandable exchange information and a consistent request process.

Our Vision

Our vision is to develop MoneyExchangeTaka into an easy-to-use platform with a broader range of supported payment methods, improved request tools and a better experience across mobile and desktop devices.

06 / SECURITY & TRANSPARENCY

Careful information. Clear expectations.

Customers should review all exchange details before sending money or digital assets. Always verify the recipient account, wallet address, amount, currency and network against the instructions provided for the specific request.

Never share your password, one-time passcode, private key or recovery phrase with anyone. Contact support through the details published on this website if anything about a request or payment instruction is unclear.

Rates, limits, fees, processing times and supported methods may vary. Check the applicable terms and final instructions before payment. A request should not be considered approved or completed until its status has been updated accordingly.

07 / FUTURE GOALS

Building a more capable platform

We plan to keep improving the platform as its services develop. Future improvements may include additional payment methods and currencies, clearer exchange-rate information, enhanced request tracking, customer account features, referral tools and improved administration workflows.

These are development goals, not a statement that every feature is currently available. Updates will be reflected on the website as features become ready for use.
