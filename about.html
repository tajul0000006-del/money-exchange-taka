Our purpose

A simpler experience for digital exchange.

FastMoney is shaped around one idea: make it easier to understand the steps involved in exchanging supported digital balances.

People use different digital wallets, payment services and currencies in everyday life. Moving between those options can feel complicated when information is unclear or the process is difficult to follow.

FastMoney brings supported exchange options into one place, so users can review the available route, understand the displayed details and submit a request through a straightforward flow.

Rates, limits, fees, availability and payment instructions may change. Each request is subject to review and manual verification before completion. Only services shown as active on the platform should be considered currently available.

What guides us

Built around clarity, care and choice.

These principles shape how we present exchange information and how we want the experience to feel.

Clarity first

We aim to make rates, fees, limits and payment instructions easy to review before a request is submitted.

More flexibility

Our goal is to support a growing range of wallets, payment services and currencies as they become available.

Careful by design

We encourage users to check recipient details, amounts, networks and instructions before sending funds.

The process

Know what to expect at every step.

A simple overview of how an exchange request works on FastMoney.

01

Choose an option

Select a supported wallet, currency or exchange direction shown on the platform.

02

Review the details

Check the displayed rate, amount, limits, fees and payment instructions.

03

Submit your request

Enter the required information carefully and submit your exchange request.

04

Verification

Your request is reviewed and manually verified before it can be completed.

Stay informed. Keep your account details private.

Always verify the recipient, amount, wallet and network before sending funds. FastMoney will never need you to share your password, PIN, OTP or account recovery codes with another person.
