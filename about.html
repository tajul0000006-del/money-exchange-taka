ABOUT FASTMONEY

One Platform.
More Ways to Move Money.

FastMoney is built to make supported digital wallet and currency exchanges easier to understand and access.

Our Purpose

FastMoney was created with a simple vision: people should be able to use the digital wallets and payment methods they already have, and exchange supported balances without unnecessary complexity. We are building a convenient place to request digital money exchanges with clear information and a straightforward process.

Buy, Sell and Exchange

Users can submit buy, sell and exchange requests involving the payment methods and digital currencies currently listed on our platform. Availability, rates, limits, fees and payment instructions are shown during the request process and may change. Each request is subject to review and manual verification before completion.

More Wallets, One Place

Our goal is to support a growing range of digital wallets, payment services and currencies. We want users to have more flexibility when moving between supported payment systems, while keeping the process understandable and transparent.

Our Global Vision

Global money transfer and broader international payment services are part of our long-term vision. These services are not represented as currently available unless they are specifically listed as active on the platform. Any future service will be introduced only when operational and legal requirements are met.

How We Work

FastMoney aims to provide clear exchange details, visible payment instructions, request tracking and customer support. Please verify the selected wallet, account details, amount, network and fees before sending funds. Never share your PIN, password, OTP or account recovery codes with anyone.

Our mission: Make digital money exchange simpler today and help connect more wallets and payment options over time.
