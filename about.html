ABOUT MONEYEXCHANGETAKA

About Our Platform

A simple digital exchange platform made for Bangladesh.

Who We Are

MoneyExchangeTaka is a Bangladesh-focused online platform developed to make digital exchange services easier to access and understand. We aim to provide a clear, convenient experience for people using digital payment methods.

What We Do

We provide a place to view supported exchange options and submit exchange requests involving services such as bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, ePay, TunePay and USD, subject to current availability. Requests are reviewed manually, and an exchange is completed only after confirmation.

Our commitment: Clear information, a simple process and responsible service for every customer.
